Current role

Koni Treats

Since September 2026 I am the content creator and social media manager for this Lagos food and celebration brand. I film, direct, script, edit, design and plan, and I appear on camera. The goal is more online orders.

FilmingDirectingScriptsVideo editingGraphicsOn-cameraReporting

What I found

  • Mostly raw phone footage with real personality: packing, deliveries and a Content Creator vs CEO banter series
  • The note-card birthday videos were the most emotional format
  • Frozen chops and party packs were under-posted for how strong the offers are
  • Posting was reactive, so network delays created gaps

The system I built

  • One batch filming day each Monday covers the following week
  • One raw clip becomes a reel, a story and a text-overlay repost
  • A fixed theme for every day so the week covers rescue, celebration, process and ordering
  • One simple order call to action on every post

Hooks written for how people talk

POV: your guests just texted "we're outside" and your house has nothing ready
When you want your birthday to look planned but you started thinking about it yesterday
POV: you said "small chops" and now the whole party wants your caterer's number

Growth plan for more orders

  • Sell the feeling, not the product list
  • Push frozen chops and party packs harder
  • Repost customer reactions every week
  • Lead with urgency the brand already owns
  • Local content and collaborations around Egbeda

Three frameworks, one voice

Sample captions written for this portfolio to show how I structure copy.

PAS · Education

Problem. Your child reads slowly and homework ends in tears.

Agitate. The longer it goes on, the more school feels like a place they are bad at.

Solve. One-to-one online tutors, from your living room, at a pace your child can keep.

Book a free assessment. Link in bio.

AIDA · Celebration food

Attention. Birthday tomorrow and no cake yet?

Interest. Celebration combos in vanilla, chocolate and red velvet, in any colour you like.

Desire. Picture the table: cake, small chops and a candle already on top.

Action. Message us on WhatsApp and book your slot.

Hook and teach · Beauty

Hook. Shiny by noon does not mean your skin is oily.

It can mean your skin is dehydrated. Oily skin has too much oil. Dehydrated skin lacks water.

Three signs: tightness after cleansing, fine lines when you smile, uneven makeup.

Save this and check tonight.
